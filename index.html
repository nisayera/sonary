A tour of Sonary

01

Turn on only what you use.

Ten modules, one switch each. A module that's off doesn't load, doesn't run in the background and never asks for permissions.

02

See what your Mac is doing.

CPU, GPU, memory, network, disk, battery and temperatures, with history graphs, the busiest apps and alerts for sustained load.

03

Never lose a copy again.

Clipboard history for text, images and files, with search and pinning. Plain-text paste on ⌘⌥⇧V, tracking junk stripped from links.

Windows

Halves, quarters, thirds, maximize and center on ⌃⌥ shortcuts. A switcher with live previews, and Dock previews across Spaces.

Tools

Command bar, offline text recognition, color picker, screenshots with an editor and scrolling capture, screen recording, scratchpad.

Mouse

Linear or smooth scrolling, acceleration off, scroll direction independent of the trackpad, button mapping, double-click filter.

App Management

Uninstaller that finds leftovers, cleaner, force quit, port manager and Homebrew updates.

Sound

Switch output and input, mute the mic on ⌃⌥M, see which apps are playing, keep the Mac awake.

Display

Brightness per display with shortcuts, and software dimming where macOS offers no control.

No account. No telemetry. No Pro tier. Just the source code.

Sonary works offline. It goes online only to check for its own updates, and for the app update checker and speed test when you ask.

Accessibility or Screen Recording access is requested only when you enable a module that needs it.

Questions, answered.

Is it really free?

Yes. Sonary is GPL-3.0-or-later open source, with no subscription and no paid tier.

Which Macs does it run on?

Macs with Apple Silicon on macOS 26 Tahoe or newer. Intel Macs and older macOS versions aren't supported.

Why does macOS warn me when I open it?

Sonary isn't notarized by Apple yet. Right-click the app and choose Open once; after that it launches normally.

Will ten modules slow my Mac down?

A disabled module doesn't load and doesn't run in the background. Turn on only what you use.

Is it finished?

Sonary is in early development. All ten modules ship today, and every milestone is its own release until 1.0. The feature checklist shows what's done and what's open.

What languages does it speak?

English and German.
